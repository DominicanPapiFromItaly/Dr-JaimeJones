    Dental Implants

    The ultimate solution to restore your smile

    What are dental implants?

    Dental implants are artificial titanium roots placed in the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They provide a strong and durable base for crowns, bridges, or dentures, restoring both aesthetics and functionality.

    Single Implants

    Used to replace a single missing tooth. The implant acts as a root and supports an individual crown, offering a natural look and full function.

    Implant-Supported Bridges

    When several consecutive teeth are missing, two or more implants are placed to support a fixed bridge. It is a stable and aesthetic solution to restore the smile.

    Full Dentures on Implants

    For patients who have lost all teeth in an arch, several implants are placed to support a complete fixed or removable denture. This ensures stability and comfort.
