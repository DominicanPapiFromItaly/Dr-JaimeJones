Advanced Endodontics

Root canal treatments with maximum precision and modern technology

What is advanced endodontics?

Advanced endodontics is the treatment that allows saving damaged or infected teeth through cleaning and sealing of the root canals. Thanks to modern technology and certified specialists, we provide safe and effective procedures.

Conventional Endodontics

Root canal treatment to eliminate infection and save the tooth. Includes cleaning, disinfection, and hermetic sealing of the root canal.

Endodontic Retreatment

Correction of previous treatments that were not successful. The defective filling is removed and a new sealing is performed with modern techniques.

Surgical Endodontics (Apicoectomy)

Surgical procedure to treat persistent infections in the root. The apex is accessed and the infected tissue is resected, preserving the tooth.

Endodontics in Multirooted Teeth

Specialized treatment for molars with multiple roots and canals. Advanced techniques and dental microscope are used to locate all canals.

Endodontics with Microscope

Treatments performed with magnification for maximum precision. Allows detection of hidden canals and improves clinical success.
