Preventive Dentistry

Take care of your smile before problems appear

What is preventive dentistry?

Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining oral health and avoiding the onset of dental diseases. Through regular check-ups and simple treatments, we help preserve healthy teeth and gums throughout life.

Dental Prophylaxis

Professional cleaning that removes plaque and tartar, preventing cavities and gum disease. It is recommended at least once a year.

Fluoride Application

Treatment that strengthens tooth enamel and reduces the risk of cavities, especially recommended for children and adolescents.

Pit and Fissure Sealants

Protective coating applied to the surfaces of molars to prevent bacteria buildup and cavities in hard-to-reach areas.

Radiographic Control

Periodic X-rays that help detect hidden problems such as interproximal cavities, infections, or developmental abnormalities.

Education and Hygiene Habits

Personalized guidance on brushing techniques, flossing, and healthy habits that help maintain daily oral health.
